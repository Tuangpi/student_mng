# MVP Requirements

## User Roles
- SaaS Super Admin
- School Admin
- Principal
- Teacher
- Accountant
- Parent
- Student

## Core Modules
1. Authentication and role-based permissions
2. School and subscription management
3. Academic years, terms, classes, and subjects
4. Student admissions and student profiles
5. Guardian management
6. Teacher profiles and assignments
7. Attendance management
8. Examinations, grading, and report cards
9. Fee structures, invoices, payments, and outstanding balances
10. Parent and student portals
11. Announcements and notifications
12. Academic and financial reports

## Myanmar-Specific Requirements
- Burmese and English interfaces
- Myanmar Unicode support
- MMK currency
- Configurable academic years and grading systems
- Printable school documents
- Record local payment methods, including cash, bank transfers, KBZPay, WavePay, and AYA Pay
- Responsive design and low-bandwidth usability

## Acceptance Criteria
- Each school can access only its own data.
- Users can access only features permitted by their roles.
- Students can be assigned to classes and academic years.
- Teachers can record attendance and examination results.
- Accountants can manage invoices and record payments.
- Parents can view only their linked children's permitted information.
- Reports can be filtered and exported or printed.
- Important actions and financial changes are auditable.

Do not claim payment integration is complete when only manual payment recording is implemented.
